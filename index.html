I Wish I Could Fly

Ronald Webel · Illustrated by Justine Martin

Redgie wants more than anything to fly. There's just one problem: no matter how hard he tries, he can't seem to get off the ground!

While the other birds soar through the sky, Redgie keeps trying, learning and asking for help. There are wobbles, crashes and plenty of bananas along the way, but Redgie is determined not to give up.

A heartwarming and funny story about believing in yourself, accepting that we all learn differently, and discovering that the things that seem impossible sometimes just take a little more courage, help and determination.

Forgiveness Under a Gum Tree

Ron Webel

Growing up in a small town with a stutter, Ron found it hard to make close friends. But he had four, and most afternoons they'd sit together in a field under a gum tree, talking about everything and nothing.

Then one day Ron overheard those friends making fun of the way he spoke. His heart sank and he wanted to run and hide, until he remembered something his dad used to say:

"Holding onto hurt is like holding onto an ember. It hurts you, but not the other person."

A gentle, true-to-life story about hurt, healing and the freedom that comes with forgiveness.

Best Day Ever

Ronald Webel

Step into the shoes of young Ron Webel, a boy with a stutter and a heart full of doubts. School is tough enough, but going on his birthday? Certain no one will celebrate him, Ron braces for a day he hopes will pass quickly.

But life has surprises in store. Through unexpected challenges and heartwarming moments, Ron discovers that sometimes the world sees us in ways we don't see ourselves.

Full of humour, courage and tender lessons, Best Day Ever is a reminder that every voice deserves to be heard, and every birthday deserves a little kindness.

About the author

Meet Ron Webel

Ron grew up in a small Australian town with a stutter. School wasn't always easy and friendships could be hard to find, but those years taught him about courage, kindness and the power of letting go of hurt.

Today Ron turns those experiences into warm, funny stories for children, so that every young reader who has ever felt different knows their voice matters.

"Every voice deserves to be heard."

Bring Ron's stories home

Perfect for bedtime, the classroom, or any child who needs a reminder that they're braver than they think.
